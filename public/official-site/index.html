Made for business owners

One app for your website, sales, and business decisions

Craft brings three everyday business jobs together: creating a website, recording sales in person, and understanding how the business is doing. You do not need to build a website from scratch. Add your shop information and products in the app, choose a storefront design, and preview the result.

When the website is ready, publish it so customers can browse your products and place an order through the available checkout. At your counter, use POS to record a separate in-person sale. Craft's reports help you review the information you have recorded, and its AI helper can explain business screens when that service is available.

How it works

  1. Create your shop websiteChoose a design, add your business details, and build your product catalogue.
  2. Preview, then publishCheck your website before making your latest changes visible to customers.
  3. Record sales and learnUse POS for counter sales and review reports as you add business records.

Build your website

Create a customer-facing website from your phone. Add products, prices, photos, categories, and pages, then preview and publish your shop when it is ready.

  • Choose an available storefront design
  • Add and organize products and categories
  • Edit your shop details and pages
  • Publish updates when you are ready

Run your shop with POS

Use Craft as your counter register. Record sales, payments, receipts, expenses, customer balances, and till activity.

  • Keep products and stock counts
  • Record full or partial payments
  • Track expenses and cash movements
  • Review sales and stock reports

Understand what is happening

Turn the records you enter into clear reports about sales, products, expenses, stock, and customer balances. Ask Craft's AI helper to explain a business screen when it is available.

  • Compare sales across time periods
  • See popular products and low stock
  • Review recorded costs and expenses
  • Use AI answers as suggestions, not guarantees
